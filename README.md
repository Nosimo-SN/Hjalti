# Hjalti

A husky companion for Skyrim with a dog's mind, built on [SkyrimNet](https://fateless.ai).

Hjalti doesn't talk. He barks, whines and growls out loud; speak to him over SkyrimNet's mind-link (telepathy) and you
hear his thoughts in plain words. What he does comes from **Canine Drives**, the SKSE plugin and SkyrimNet layer in this
repository: needs that rise and fall with time, and the dog things he does about them on his own.

> **Pre-release.** This is an early public build. Expect rough edges and keep your saves backed up.
> Download the latest build from [Releases](https://github.com/Nosimo-SN/Hjalti/releases).

## What he does

- **Needs:** hunger, affection, restlessness, tiredness and missing you, each rising and falling with game time and with
  what happens. They shape his prompt and what he chooses to do.
- **On his own, when it's quiet:** begs, naps (on his side, chin on his paws, or curled up tight in the cold), plays,
  play bows, wiggles with joy, rolls right over, sniffs the ground, the air and the people he meets, sneezes, cocks his
  head at odd sounds, howls with bards, scavenges or steals food, hunts game he can take and eats what he catches.
- **Senses:** he smells threats and prey before you see them, and in a fight he knows who is doing what to whom.
- **Learning and memory:** he learns from praise and scolding, dreams when he sleeps, and greets you after you've been
  apart.
- **Growth:** he levels with you, and runes work into his coat as he witnesses dragon souls taken, each step bringing a
  gift: Frost Bite, Perseverance, Frost Step and Frost Howl.
- **With Frostfall:** he presses close and warms you when you're cold.

## Requirements

- Skyrim Special Edition / AE with Dawnguard
- [SKSE](https://skse.silverlock.org/) and [Address Library for SKSE Plugins](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/32444)
- SkyrimNet 1.1 or later
- [SeverActions](https://github.com/Severause/SeverActions) (his running about, sniffing and hunting use its travel package)
- [PapyrusUtil SE](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/13048)
- [Open Animation Replacer](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/92109) (his own animations)

Optional: Frostfall.

## Installing

Install the release zip as one mod with Mod Organizer 2 or Vortex. Hjalti waits outside the Sleeping Giant Inn in
Riverwood; ask him to come along through SkyrimNet. A new game is the cleanest start, but an existing save works.

Settings are on the SkyrimNet dashboard: **Settings > Plugins > Canine Drives**. Changes apply within a few seconds.
The release's `README.txt` walks through the ones worth knowing (food theft, carcass meat, progression, debug).

## Reporting problems

[Open an issue](https://github.com/Nosimo-SN/Hjalti/issues) with what happened and roughly when, a copy of
`Documents\My Games\Skyrim Special Edition\SKSE\CanineDrives.log` (rewritten each launch, so copy it before restarting),
and your SkyrimNet version.

## For other mods: the C API

`CanineDrives.dll` exports a small, versioned, read-only C API (see [`include/CanineDrivesAPI.h`](include/CanineDrivesAPI.h)):
`CDAPI_GetVersion()` and `CDAPI_GetSnapshot(actorRefID, output, outputSize)` return his hunger and tiredness, their
tiers and warning flags, and what he's doing right now (napping, eating, hunting, playing, fighting and so on). Plain
fixed-width fields, safe from any thread; resolve the exports with `GetProcAddress`.

## Building from source

- Visual Studio 2022 Build Tools (C++), CMake, Ninja, [vcpkg](https://github.com/microsoft/vcpkg)
  (`VCPKG_ROOT` set; triplet `x64-windows-static-md`)
- CommonLibSSE-NG is the `lib/commonlibsse-ng` submodule (`git submodule update --init --recursive`), or point
  `COMMONLIB_DIR` at an existing checkout.

```bash
cmake -B build -G Ninja -DCMAKE_BUILD_TYPE=RelWithDebInfo -DCMAKE_TOOLCHAIN_FILE=%VCPKG_ROOT%/scripts/buildsystems/vcpkg.cmake -DVCPKG_TARGET_TRIPLET=x64-windows-static-md
cmake --build build
```

Run from a Visual Studio developer shell. The SkyrimNet layer is in `skyrimnet/nosimo.canine_drives`; the settings page
is generated by `tools/gen_settings.py`. Hjalti's plugin, meshes, textures, animations and sounds ship in the release
zip, not in this repository.
